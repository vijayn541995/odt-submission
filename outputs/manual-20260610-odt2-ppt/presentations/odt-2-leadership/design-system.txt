Diagram grammar:
- Rounded rectangles with 1-2px lines.
- Red for governance/approval current gates.
- Blue for platform/backend/evidence.
- Green for readiness/passed state.
- Orange/cream for callouts and human review.
- Arrows only when direction matters.

Footer grammar:
- Top-left: "Confidential - Oracle Restricted (Employees Only)" on interior slides.
- Bottom-left slide number.
- Bottom-right red square with white O-style mark matching source deck flavor.

Allowed layout families:
- Cover with right-side product mark.

- Split explanation + diagram.
- Full-width workflow canvas.
- Dark technical architecture canvas.
- Roadmap bands.
- Closing summary columns + callout.

Banned motifs:
- Generic SaaS card grids repeated on every slide.
- Text-heavy slide dumps.
